About this lab

What this platform contains and why it exists.

🎓 The Academy

Five gated levels from zero to professional. Every module has lessons + a knowledge check; every level ends in an exam. Pass 80% to unlock the next level. Your progress is saved in your browser — no account needed.

💻 The Terminal Trainer

A real-feeling Linux terminal in your browser with a virtual filesystem. Practice pwd, ls, cd, cat, grep, find, nmap and more with guided missions that award XP — before you ever touch the actual VM.

🧰 The Tool Reference

The 12 core lab tools — what each does, when you unlock it, and ready-to-copy commands for the real Kali VM.

🖥️ The Real Lab

A genuine Kali Linux VM on this machine's D: drive, built by one script: scripts\setup-lab.ps1. Verified download, snapshot-protected, fully legal targets only.
