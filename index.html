About this lab

What this platform contains and why it exists.

🎓 The Academy

Five gated levels from zero to professional. Every module has lessons + a knowledge check; every level ends in an exam. Pass 80% to unlock the next level. Progress syncs to your account when you're signed in — saved in your browser otherwise.

A real-feeling Linux terminal in your browser with a virtual filesystem. Practice pwd, ls, cd, cat, grep, find, nmap and more with guided missions that award XP — before you ever touch the actual VM.
